Frequently Asked Questions

🔹 Is BESTHASH really free?

Yes! You can start mining with zero upfront cost. We only charge a small infrastructure fee.

🔹 How do I receive my mined crypto?

After each 20-minute session, you'll be prompted to enter your wallet address and pay the network fee to receive your earnings.

🔹 What makes your platform different?

We use real enterprise GPU clusters, not simulations. Our 99.9% uptime and 24/7 operations ensure consistent mining rewards.

🔹 Can I mine multiple coins at once?

Currently, you can mine one coin per session. Switch anytime between our 10 supported cryptocurrencies.

🔹 How much can I realistically earn?

Earnings depend on cluster power and coin selection. Max Power mode on BTC can yield up to 0.000045 BTC per second.

🔹 Is there a minimum withdrawal?

No minimum! Whatever you mine is yours, minus the infrastructure fee.
